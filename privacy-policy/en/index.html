Privacy policy

This policy explains, in plain language, what information the Kipu app handles, where it is stored, why, and when it may leave your device.

Draft under review. The controller's details (legal identity, address, contact and jurisdiction) are still missing. Items highlighted in amber will be completed before this policy is published.

01 Introduction

Kipu is an app to record, organize and understand your personal finances and those of your business, in separate books. It works without user accounts: there is no sign-up, no sign-in and no Kipu server where your information is stored.

  • Your data stays on your device Kipu stores your financial information in the app's private storage. It has no servers of its own and no sync.
  • No account You don't need to sign up or sign in to use Kipu.
  • Voice, receipts and import on your device Speech recognition, receipt reading and bank statement import are processed on the device. Audio and images are neither stored nor sent.
  • Google Play handles purchases Kipu talks to Google Play to check, buy or restore Premium. Your transactions are never part of that communication.
  • You decide which copies leave The only full copy of your data that can leave the device is the one you export and share. Android's automatic backup is turned off for Kipu.
  • You can delete everything You can delete all data from Settings. Copies you saved outside Kipu are yours to delete (section 12).

Information Kipu does not ask for

Kipu does not ask for your email address, phone number, postal address, ID document, date of birth, contacts, location or health data. It has no general access to your camera or photos: it only receives the photo you take or the image you pick when you scan a receipt (section 07).

Other people's data. Bank statements and receipts may include names of merchants or people, tax ID numbers or transaction references. If you import or confirm them, they are stored in your transactions like any other data you type.
A note on the data schema. The database includes client tables (name and contact detail) and invoice tables that, in this version, have no data-entry screen in the app. If they were enabled, this policy would be updated first, because those fields could contain third-party data.

05 Purposes of processing

Information is used only to:

  • show your accounts, balances, transactions and reminders;
  • calculate totals, statistics and comparisons from your own data, on the device;
  • turn what you dictate, scan or import into transactions you review before saving;
  • detect possible duplicates and suggest categories;
  • schedule and show payment and collection reminders;
  • protect access to the app with a PIN or fingerprint;
  • remember your preferences;
  • handle the purchase and restoration of Premium;
  • create a backup when you ask for one and restore it when you decide to.

Kipu does not use your data for advertising, commercial profiling or sale to third parties.

06 Local storage and offline use

Financial information is stored in an SQLite database inside the app's private storage. Under normal use, other apps on the device cannot read that file.

  • Data is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Recording, browsing, statistics, voice entry, receipt scanning and statement import work without an Internet connection.
  • A connection is only involved in the Google Play operations described in section 08.

07 Voice, receipts and bank statement import

These three features turn information into transactions. In every case you see a draft and decide what to save. Nothing is sent off the device.

Speech recognition

  • The microphone is used only after you tap the voice button and while you record (up to 15 seconds). Android asks for permission the first time.
  • Audio stays in the device's memory during recording and transcription. It is not written to files or sent anywhere.
  • Transcription runs on the device with the sherpa-onnx engine and the Spanish NVIDIA NeMo FastConformer model (CC BY 4.0 license), which ships with the app. No speech service from Google, the device maker or any third party is used.
  • The first time, Kipu copies the model (about 130 MB) to its private storage and checks its integrity. That copy contains no personal data and needs no connection.
  • Only the transaction you confirm is saved, not the audio or the transcript.

Receipt scanning

  • You can take a photo with the system camera app or pick an image. Kipu does not request the camera permission: Android takes the photo and hands Kipu only that image.
  • Text is recognized on the device with Tesseract OCR and a Spanish language model included in the app.
  • Kipu suggests details such as merchant, date, amounts, taxes, RUC number (Peruvian tax ID), items and payment method. You can correct them, and only what you confirm is saved.
  • The image is not kept: the file handed over by the system and the temporary files are deleted when reading finishes, is cancelled or fails. If the system closed the app while you were using the camera, a temporary file could remain in Kipu's private cache, which Android may delete.

Bank statement import

  • You can import CSV, Excel (XLSX) or text-based PDF files that you pick with the system file picker. Compatibility depends on the bank and the file format.
  • Kipu does not connect to your bank and does not ask for your banking credentials. If the PDF has a password, it is used only to open the file and is not stored.
  • The content is processed in memory. Before importing, you review the detected transactions and possible duplicates. Kipu saves the transactions you confirm, a record of the import and, where relevant, rules to suggest categories.
  • Kipu does not keep the imported file. Android's file picker may leave a temporary copy in the app's private cache; Android may delete it, and it disappears when you uninstall Kipu or clear its storage.
  • You can undo an entire import from the import history.

08 Network communication

Kipu's code does not talk to any servers of its own and does not send financial information to external services. There is no sync, no Kipu cloud backup, no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or telemetry.

Even so, the app is not fully cut off from the network. These are the known cases, and none of them transmits your transactions, balances, accounts or reminders:

  1. Google Play Billing. When the app opens, Kipu connects to the Google Play billing service on the device to learn the status of your purchases. When you buy or restore Premium, Google Play processes the operation with your Google account and transaction details. Kipu only stores whether Premium is active.
  2. Components of the Google Play library. The Google billing library included in the app bundles Google components that declare the Internet and network-state permissions, and that Google may use to send technical information about how billing works. Kipu adds no financial data to that information and does not receive it.
  3. Files you share. When you share a backup, the app you choose (for example, email or messaging) sends it, according to its own policy.

Speech recognition, receipt reading and statement import do not use the network.

11 Backups, export and restore

Backups you export

A backup is created only when you request it from Settings. Kipu does not send it to any server. It is a JSON file with books, accounts, categories, transactions, transfers, reminders, shopping lists, currencies, exchange rates, import history, classification rules and the client and invoice tables (usually empty). It does not include your PIN.

  • The file is neither encrypted nor signed. Anyone or any app that obtains it can read its contents.
  • Besides the copy you share, Kipu keeps the file in its private storage with a name similar to mi_wallet_backup_<number>.json and does not delete it automatically.
  • When you share it, the file is handed to the app or service you choose. From then on, that service's policy applies.

Restore

When restoring a file, Kipu checks its whole structure before changing the database. If validation fails, no data is changed. If it is valid, the backup replaces all current information. The file is not removed from its original location.

Android automatic backup and device-to-device transfer

Since version 1.0.4, Kipu excludes its database, preferences and files from Android's automatic backup to your Google account and from direct device-to-device transfer. To move your data to another device, export a backup and restore it there.

Earlier versions. Test versions before 1.0.4 allowed Android's automatic backup. If you had it turned on, an older Kipu backup may exist in your Google account. Google manages that backup, and you can review or delete it from the backup settings of your Google account.
Recommendation. Keep backups somewhere safe, share them only through trusted channels and delete the ones you no longer need.

12 Data retention and deletion

Data is kept on your device while the app is installed and you have not deleted it. Kipu keeps no copies on servers of its own.

What you can delete in the app

  • transactions and transfers;
  • reminders, which also cancels their notification;
  • categories you created, together with their subcategories; transactions that used them become uncategorized;
  • shopping lists and their items; expenses already created are kept;
  • entire imports, from the import history, together with the transactions they added;
  • learned classification rules.

Delete all data

Go to Settings → Data and security → "Eliminar todos los datos" (Delete all data). When you confirm, Kipu:

  • deletes books, accounts, transactions, transfers, categories, reminders, shopping lists, import history, classification rules, exchange rates, clients and invoices;
  • cancels scheduled notifications;
  • erases the PIN, failed attempts and the fingerprint preference;
  • resets the initial setup, the welcome screens and the tour;
  • keeps only the theme preference and the local Premium flag.

The database deletion is transactional: if an error occurs, the database keeps its previous state. If a later step fails, the app reports a partial result and lets you try again.

What "Delete all data" does not remove

  • backups saved in Kipu's private storage and any you shared or saved outside the app;
  • temporary files the file picker may have left in the app's private cache;
  • the prepared copy of the voice model, which contains no personal data;
  • your Google Play purchases: Premium can be restored later;
  • an Android backup created by versions before 1.0.4 (section 11).

How to remove what remains

  • To erase everything Kipu keeps on the device, including backups saved in its private storage, use Android's option to clear the app's storage (in the Kipu app info) or uninstall it.
  • Backups you shared are deleted in each app or service where they are.
  • An older Android backup is deleted from the backup settings of your Google account.

Current limitations

  • Accounts (cash, bank, card) can be edited but not deleted individually in this version.
  • Backup files cannot be deleted from the app.

There are no user accounts to delete. If you need help erasing your information, write to [CONTACT EMAIL PENDING].

13 Information security

Measures in the current version:

  • storage inside the app's private area;
  • optional PIN lock: the PIN itself is not stored; a salted hash (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256) is kept in Android secure storage, with increasing wait times after several failed attempts;
  • optional fingerprint unlock, verified by Android: Kipu never receives your biometric data;
  • full validation of backups before restoring them, and replacement in a single operation;
  • receipt images and audio are deleted after processing;
  • diagnostic logs without financial information: they only describe how the voice model is prepared;
  • no financial data is sent over the network.

Limitations you should know about:

  • The database is not encrypted. Android's app isolation protects it; the PIN locks the app but does not encrypt the data.
  • Backups are not encrypted.
  • Reminder titles may be visible in notifications, including on the lock screen, depending on your device settings.

No measure removes risk entirely. Physical access to an unlocked device and backups circulating through insecure channels are the main exposure scenarios.

14 Children's privacy

Kipu is a personal finance tool not directed at children under 13. The app does not ask for a date of birth or perform age checks.

Because Kipu sends no information to the controller and creates no user accounts, no data about children is collected on servers. If a child uses the app, their information stays on the device, like anyone else's.

Parents or guardians can delete that information by following the steps in section 12.

15 Your rights and privacy questions

Because your data is on your device and not on the controller's servers, you exercise access, rectification and deletion directly in the app:

  • access: the app's screens show your data;
  • rectification: you can edit accounts, transactions, categories and reminders;
  • deletion: you can delete records or all data (section 12);
  • portability: you can export a JSON backup (section 11).

The controller cannot view, export or delete your data on your behalf, because it has no access to it.
