Privacy policy

This policy explains, in plain language, what information the Kipu app handles, where it is stored, for what purpose, and in which cases it may leave your device.

Draft under review. The controller details are still pending (legal identity, address, contact email and jurisdiction). Sections marked in amber will be completed before publication.

01 Presentation

Kipu is an application for recording and organising personal finances. It runs without user accounts: there is no sign-up, no sign-in, and no Kipu server where your information is stored.

In three points

  • Your financial data is stored on your device.
  • Kipu has no servers of its own and no synchronisation between devices.
  • Information leaves the device only when you export a backup, or through services of the operating system and the app store (see sections 07 and 08).

Information Kipu does not request

Kipu does not request or store email address, phone number, postal address, identity document, contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or health data. It does not request a date of birth.

Note about the data schema. The database includes tables for clients (name and contact detail) and invoices that have no input screen in this version of the app. If they are enabled, this policy will be updated before publication, as those fields could contain third-party data.

05 Purposes of processing

Your information is used only to:

  • show your accounts, balances, transactions and reminders;
  • calculate totals, charts and projections from your own data, on the device;
  • schedule and show payment reminders;
  • remember your usage preferences;
  • manage the purchase and restoration of the subscription;
  • create the backup when you request it and restore it when you decide.

Kipu does not use your data for advertising, commercial profiling, sale to third parties or the creation of profiles on servers.

06 Local storage and offline operation

All financial information is stored in a SQLite database inside the application's private storage. Under normal use, other apps on the device cannot read that file.

  • Data persists across restarts of the device and is kept until you delete it or uninstall the application.
  • Recording and consulting information work without an internet connection.
  • A connection is only needed to purchase or restore the subscription, and for backups managed by the operating system.

07 Communications and transmission of information

The code of Kipu contains no calls to servers of our own and sends no financial information to external services. There is no synchronisation between devices, no cloud backup by Kipu and no usage telemetry.

There are three situations involving network communication. None of them transmits your transactions, balances, accounts or reminders:

  1. Purchase or restoration of the subscription. Kipu queries the app store for the product identifier and the information needed to process the payment. The store receives that data and the outcome of the operation; Kipu only stores whether the purchase is active.
  2. Operating system backup. If Android backup is enabled, the system may back up the private data of applications, including the Kipu database, to your Google account. That backup is controlled by the operating system and by Google, not by Kipu, and you can disable it in your device settings.
  3. Browser builds (not published). Browser builds, which today are used only for internal validation and are not distributed, load resources of the Flutter engine from Google servers.

Network permission. The application declares internet access because it is required by the Google Play purchase component. Kipu does not use that permission to send financial information.

09 Backups, export and import

A backup is created only when you request it from Settings. Kipu does not send the file to any server.

What the backup contains

The backup is a JSON file containing:

  • books and their base currencies;
  • accounts, categories, transactions and transfers;
  • reminders and their dates;
  • shopping lists and their items;
  • exchange rates and currencies;
  • client and invoice tables, which have no input screen in this version and are normally empty.

What you should know

  • The file is not encrypted or digitally signed. Anyone or any app that obtains it can read its full contents.
  • In addition to the copy you share, the app keeps another copy in its private storage with a name like mi_wallet_backup_<number>.json. Kipu does not delete it automatically.
  • When you share it, the file is handed to the app or service you choose (for example messaging or email). From that moment it is governed by that service's policy.
  • Total deletion of Kipu data does not delete these exported files or any copies you have shared.

Import and restoration

When importing a file, Kipu validates its entire structure before modifying the database. If validation fails, no data is changed. If it is valid, the backup replaces all current information. The imported file is not deleted from its original location.

Recommendation. Keep backups in a safe place, share them only through trusted channels and delete the ones you no longer need.

10 Retention and deletion of data

Data is kept on your device while the app is installed and until you delete it. Kipu keeps no copies on its own servers.

What you can delete from the app

  • transactions, one by one;
  • transfers;
  • reminders, which also cancel their notification;
  • categories, together with their subcategories; transactions that used them become uncategorised;
  • shopping lists and their items; expenses already created are kept as history.

Total deletion of data

Kipu includes an option in Settings → Data & security → “Delete all data”. When you confirm it, the app:

  • deletes books, accounts, transactions, transfers, categories, clients, invoices, reminders, shopping lists and other local records;
  • cancels scheduled notifications;
  • resets the onboarding and navigation state;
  • keeps the colour theme preference and the local Premium indicator;
  • keeps backup files exported previously and any copy saved outside the app.

The operation runs as a transaction: if an error occurs, the database keeps its previous state. If a later step fails, the app reports that the result is partial and allows retrying.

Total deletion does not cancel purchases or subscriptions managed by Google Play. The Premium right can be checked or restored through the app's existing mechanism.

The operation does not delete copies managed by Android Auto Backup or files shared with other services; those copies follow the lifecycle of Android and of the service that received them.

Current limitations

  • Financial accounts you create (cash, bank, card) can be edited, but not deleted individually, from the app in this version.
  • Backup files are not deleted from the app, not even through total deletion.

Uninstall and external files

Uninstalling the app deletes the local database and the preferences on the device. It does not delete files you shared or saved outside the app (messaging, email, cloud storage, computer).

Operating system backups may be kept after uninstalling the app, depending on device settings and Google's policies.

There are no user accounts to delete. If you need help deleting all your information, write to [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL PENDING].

11 Security of information

Measures implemented in the current version:

  • storage inside the application's private area, without permissions to access external storage;
  • full validation of backup files before importing them, and replacement in a single operation, so that a failed restore leaves no partial data;
  • no logging of financial information in the application's diagnostic records;
  • no transmission of financial data over the network.

Limitations you should be aware of:

  • The database has no additional encryption. It is a SQLite file protected by the operating system's isolation.
  • Backups are not encrypted.
  • The app has no password or biometric lock: anyone with the unlocked device can open it.

No measure removes risk entirely. Physical access to an unlocked device and the circulation of backups through insecure channels are the main exposure scenarios.

12 Children's privacy

Kipu is a personal finance tool not directed at children under 13. The app does not request a date of birth or perform age verification.

Because Kipu sends no information to the controller and creates no user accounts, no children's data is collected on servers. If a child uses the app, their information stays on the device, like that of any other person.

Parents, mothers or guardians can delete that information by uninstalling the app and deleting any backup files that were exported.

13 Your rights and privacy enquiries

Because your data is on your device and not on the controller's servers, you exercise access, rectification and deletion directly in the app:

  • access: the app screens show your data;
  • rectification: you can edit accounts, transactions, categories and reminders;
  • deletion: you can delete individual records (section 10).

The controller cannot look up, export or delete your data on your behalf, because it has no access to it.
